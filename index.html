Skim

New research papers, ranked for you, in an inbox you can actually finish.

Skim is a free Android app for researchers, students and anyone who follows scientific literature. It pulls new papers from open scholarly indexes, ranks them against the topics you care about, and gives you a short inbox to triage instead of an endless feed. You can read the paper, keep notes and highlights, get a plain-language summary, and export citations when you write.

What Skim does

A feed ranked for you

Pick topics, keywords and venues. Skim ranks new papers against them and shows why each one ranked where it did. Muted topics stay out.

An inbox you can clear

Mark papers read, favourite or saved for later. Skipped papers leave. The inbox gets to zero.

Read and annotate

Open the PDF in the app, highlight passages, write notes, and pick up where you left off.

Summaries

Short summaries and explanations of a paper's abstract, generated on your device or with an AI provider you choose.

Open access first

Skim finds legal open-access copies and can show only papers you can read for free. For paywalled work, it helps you email the author.

Citations and export

Copy citations as BibTeX, RIS, APA or IEEE. Export whole lists with your notes, and import from BibTeX or RIS.

Collections and sharing

Group papers into collections and share a read-only link to a collection if you want to.

Sync and backup

Sign in to sync your library across devices, or stay offline and use file backup and restore.

Where the papers come from

Skim reads public metadata from arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, OpenAlex, Unpaywall and ORCID. Skim does not host papers. Links open the publisher's or repository's own copy.
